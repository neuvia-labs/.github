## 🧩 What we're up to

Something at the intersection of neurodevelopmental care, careful AI, and the people who need both. We're not ready to show it yet. We are ready to say it's real, it's being built with clinicians rather than around them, and it ships every week.

---

## 🔍 The problems we get to work on

The interesting thing about this space is how many hard problems it hides behind one gentle mission:

- 🎥 Real-time systems where a dropped frame can mean a missed moment that mattered
- 🧠 Machine findings that must remain traceable, reviewable, and owned by a human, always
- 🕸️ A living knowledge graph for a field where the language itself is still evolving
- 🔒 Privacy engineering where "compliant" is the floor, across continents and legal regimes
- 🌍 Software that has to work just as well in Lomé as in Los Angeles, in more than one language

If reading that list made you want to argue about any of it, we'd probably enjoy meeting you.

## 📈 Where things stand

The foundations are laid and load-bearing. The loop at the heart of it all is closing, piece by piece. The waitlist of people asking "when?" is the reason we keep the lights on late.

## 🌱 Why

Because the right question at the right time can change everything.
